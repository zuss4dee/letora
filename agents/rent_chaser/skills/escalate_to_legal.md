# Skill: escalate_to_legal

## When to use

Suspected serious arrears strategy, harassment, or threats—**not** for routine first/second chasers.

## Behaviour

- Tenant-facing copy: ask them to **contact the office urgently** to discuss the account.
- Do **not** cite specific legal procedures unless explicitly provided in user instructions.
- The runtime should rely on human review before any formal notice.
